# Sociology UPSC Comparison

A quick-reference sheet for UPSC Sociology Optional, covering theory, syllabus, and thinkers in one place.

## What's inside

1. **Strand-by-strand comparison** — 15 major sociological perspectives (Positivism, Functionalism, Marxism, Weberian sociology, Feminism, Postmodernism, etc.) with core ideas, thinkers, methods, criticisms, and counter-arguments.
2. **High-value criticism pairs** — ready-made "if you write X, counter with Y" combinations for answer writing.
3. **The 4-angle framework** — Positivist / Interpretative / Conflict / Functionalist lenses for analysing any topic.
4. **UPSC analytical formula** — a worked example (religion) showing how to link thinkers into one analytical paragraph.
5. **Official UPSC syllabus** — full Paper I (Fundamentals of Sociology) and Paper II (Indian Society: Structure and Change), broken into bulleted topics.
6. **Complete thinker index** — all 44 thinkers A–Z with school and one-line UPSC-ready contribution.

## Notes

- Syllabus content is sourced from the official UPSC Sociology Optional syllabus (Paper I & II, 250 marks each).
- Built for quick revision and answer-writing practice — not a substitute for standard textbooks (Haralambos & Holborn, Ritzer, IGNOU material, etc.).
